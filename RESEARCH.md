# Racing research for LapSim

The consolidated professional-racing and Formula Student research is in **[docs/research/professional_racing](docs/research/professional_racing/README.md)**.

Start with the [repository-specific development roadmap](docs/research/professional_racing/lapsim_development_roadmap.md) for proposed improvements, dependencies and acceptance tests. The [machine-readable backlog](docs/research/professional_racing/backlog.json) contains ten proposed work packages; none is marked implemented by this research upload.

The collection covers F1, F2/F3/F4, Formula E, Formula Student/SAE Electric, and selected IndyCar, WEC/IMSA, GT and NASCAR programs. It distinguishes actual public code/data from descriptions of proprietary systems, and separates historical deployments from current installations and announcements.

Research consolidated 11 October 2026. Read the [evidence/access boundaries](docs/research/professional_racing/evidence_and_access.md) before importing models or datasets. Private team evidence, restricted tyre data and third-party source articles are not copied into this repository.
