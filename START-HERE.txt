LapSim public demonstration - Windows x64

1. Read LICENSE, LICENSE_SCOPE.md, NOTICE, and docs/licensing.md before using,
   modifying, or sharing the software. This is source-available software with
   a competition-use restriction; it is not open source.
2. Extract the ZIP, then double-click LapSim.exe. Python is bundled.
3. The public executable starts with a synthetic analytic course and contains
   no bundled team-derived GNSS/IMU course, telemetry, calibration, historical
   validation report, or output directory.
4. Choose "Synthetic loop · AI demo" from Course and run a centerline lap.
5. Click Simulator map to explore the flowchart, physics equations and
   individual variables. Search and click the child buttons to drill down.
6. Read docs/public_demo.md for the repeatable demonstration and model limits.
7. portable-self-test.json and checksums.json contain release evidence;
   build-info.json identifies the curated source snapshot.

The ZIP keeps curated public documentation, the map, a reviewed source subset,
project and dependency notices, and frozen self-test evidence together. Public
availability does not authorize another student vehicle competition program to
use Covered Material for its vehicle or competition work.

Team-derived data and reports have unresolved or separate publication rights
and are excluded from the public Windows artifact. UMD users who have separate
authorization may use those materials from access-controlled storage with the
source checkout. AI racing line and the separate four-wheel pose preview are
experimental. Test agreement does not establish real-car predictive accuracy.

This is an unsigned educational/research build. See docs/release.md for
rebuilding, source identity, licensing, distribution details, and
troubleshooting.
