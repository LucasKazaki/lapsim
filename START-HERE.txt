LapSim public demonstration - Windows x64

1. Read LICENSE, LICENSE_SCOPE.md, NOTICE, and docs/licensing.md before using,
   modifying, or sharing the software. This is source-available software with
   a competition-use restriction; it is not open source.
2. Extract the ZIP, then double-click LapSim.exe. Python is bundled.
3. The public executable starts with "Synthetic loop · AI demo". Run a
   centerline lap before trying the experimental AI path.
4. Click Simulator map to explore the flowchart, physics equations and
   individual variables. Search and click the child buttons to drill down.
5. Read docs/team_guide.md for the demo, units, saved records and model limits.
   The source engineering handoff is docs/engineering_handoff.md.
6. portable-self-test.json and verification/ contain the release check evidence.
   checksums.json identifies the executable; build-info.json identifies sources
   and the material intentionally excluded from the public archive.

The executable also runs by itself. The ZIP keeps the public documentation,
map, source snapshot, project and dependency notices, and verification evidence
together. Public availability does not authorize another student vehicle
competition program to use the covered software for its vehicle or competition
work.

The team-derived GNSS/IMU course and publication-pending analysis/output data
are not included in this public release. A separately authorized UMD checkout
may expose those resources for internal work. The public executable offers only
analytic synthetic courses. AI racing line and the separate four-wheel pose
preview are experimental. Test agreement does not establish real-car
predictive accuracy.

This is an unsigned educational/research build. See docs/release.md for
rebuilding, source identity, licensing, distribution details, and
troubleshooting.
