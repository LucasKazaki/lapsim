LapSim public demonstration - Windows x64

1. Read LICENSE, LICENSE_SCOPE.md, NOTICE, and docs/licensing.md before using,
   modifying, or sharing the software. This is source-available software with
   a competition-use restriction; it is not open source.
2. Extract the ZIP, then double-click LapSim.exe. Python is bundled.
3. The public executable starts with a synthetic analytic course and contains
   no bundled team-derived GNSS/IMU course, telemetry, calibration, or output
   directory.
4. Choose "Synthetic loop · AI demo" from Course and run a centerline lap.
5. Click Simulator map to explore the flowchart, physics equations and
   individual variables. Search and click the child buttons to drill down.
6. Read docs/team_guide.md for the demo, units, saved records and model limits.
7. portable-self-test.json and verification/ contain the release check
   evidence. checksums.json identifies the executable; build-info.json
   identifies the curated source snapshot.

The executable also runs by itself. The ZIP keeps curated documentation, map,
source, project and dependency notices, and verification evidence together.
Public availability does not authorize another student vehicle competition
program to use Covered Material for its vehicle or competition work.

The team-derived fused GNSS/IMU course has unresolved publication authority and
is excluded from the public Windows artifact. UMD users who have separate
authorization may use team data from access-controlled storage with the source
checkout. AI racing line and the separate four-wheel pose preview are
experimental. Test agreement does not establish real-car predictive accuracy.

This is an unsigned educational/research build. See docs/release.md for
rebuilding, source identity, licensing, distribution details, and
troubleshooting.
