Understand the assumptions

Static axle loads and regularized linear lateral tires, constrained by a shared longitudinal/lateral friction circle. Forward motion only. The inputs prescribe contact-force requests; there is no motor, wheel-rotation, pack or controller model in this study. These synthetic parameters are not a calibrated team car.
